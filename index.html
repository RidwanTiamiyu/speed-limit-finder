How to read this

Signs come from computer-vision detections in Mapillary photos, so some are wrong and some are missing where no one uploaded photos. A sign is compared with the nearest road lines inside the match radius. A cross street or ramp sign can trigger a false flag, so check the photo or Street View before trusting a red ring.
